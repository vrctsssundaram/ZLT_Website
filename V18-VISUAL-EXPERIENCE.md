# V18 — Signal / Silicon visual experience

## Direction
A conversion-led semiconductor design system inspired by the interaction grammar of high-engagement technology sites without copying their brand treatment.

## Visual roles
- Deep navy: engineering trust, authority and reading stability.
- Electric blue/cyan: semiconductor technology, interfaces and active signal flow.
- Violet/magenta: advanced R&D, future technology and differentiation.
- Teal: verification, proof, success and validated outcomes.
- Coral/amber: deliberately isolated conversion actions and momentum.

## Interaction system
- Animated aurora and circuit-grid hero.
- Interactive silicon-core visual with signal nodes, data lanes and orbital motion.
- Pointer parallax on desktop/fine-pointer devices.
- Card pointer-lighting and lift.
- Scroll-based reveal choreography.
- Animated proof counters.
- Continuous technology spectrum rail.
- Scroll progress indicator.
- Magnetic primary actions.
- Page-specific colour signatures for IP, engineering, applications, R&D, company, contact, news and careers.
- Dark/high-energy and light/quiet section rhythm.
- Motion automatically disabled for prefers-reduced-motion.
- Touch/coarse-pointer interfaces avoid hover-dependent behaviour.

## Conversion principle
Colour and motion create attention and hierarchy; content, technical evidence and clear CTA routes carry the buying decision. Action colours remain scarce enough to stay visually dominant.

## Dry-run safeguard
The repository remains a staging/dry-run source. noindex/nofollow and production canonical URLs remain unchanged until final migration approval.
