# V17 — Conversion-led enterprise website pass

## Objective
Turn the dry-run site from a descriptive corporate website into a technically credible buyer journey without overstating capability.

## What changed
- Homepage now starts from buyer objectives: license, define, implement, verify, prove, co-develop.
- IP page now asks for target platform and PPA context and explains the evaluation path.
- Engineering page now routes blocked programmes by stage/outcome and adds commercial engagement models.
- Applications cards now lead directly into contextual enquiries.
- R&D page now frames feasibility-to-hardware transfer as a staged engagement.
- Company page adds a conversion-oriented trust section and CEO leadership credibility.
- Contact form no longer rejects personal email domains.
- Contact form adds optional current-stage and needed-next qualification fields.
- Contact page explains triage, confidentiality, scoping and engagement flow.
- Global CTA tracking is extended to contact links.
- Search dialog restores focus and traps keyboard focus while open.
- Focus-visible, touch targets, coarse-pointer support, mobile font sizing and safe-area handling are hardened.

## Claims discipline
- Commercial IP, current engineering work, applied R&D and planned infrastructure remain separated.
- No guaranteed response-time, PPA, schedule or commercial outcome is claimed.
- Detailed PPA and protected implementation material remain subject to the relevant evaluation/NDA route.

## Production note
This repository remains the dry-run source. Canonical URLs continue to point to https://zeptologic.com and pages remain noindex/nofollow until the approved production migration.
