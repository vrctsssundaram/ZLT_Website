Bring us a research problem

Start with the result hardware has to prove.

A good co-development discussion does not begin with “we need an FPGA.” It begins with the computation, bottleneck, interface, system constraint and evidence needed to make the next technical decision.

Input
Algorithm, research result, mathematical kernel, reference model, protocol requirement or existing prototype.
Architecture
Partitioning, datapath definition, memory/data movement, interfaces, precision and hardware feasibility.
Engineering
RTL, verification, implementation and FPGA evidence where the programme calls for it.
Output
A hardware result that can support a proposal, collaboration decision, product path or further research programme.
