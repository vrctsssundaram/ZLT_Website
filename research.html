Research to measurable hardware

Architecture, RTL, verification and FPGA evidence turn algorithms and emerging system concepts into engineering programmes that can be evaluated.

Research is part of how Zepto Logic expands its owned semiconductor capability.

The R&D portfolio concentrates on compute-heavy and security-sensitive problems where hardware architecture, arithmetic, data movement and implementation choices materially change system behaviour.

Programmes are developed through internal research, government initiatives and academic collaboration, with FPGA evidence used to move promising ideas toward stronger engineering maturity.
