Technical questions

Inputs, deliverables and confidentiality.

What can we provide as input?

A specification, algorithm, reference model, interface definition or existing RTL block.

Which HDL languages are supported?

Verilog, SystemVerilog and VHDL.

What can the hand-off include?

Microarchitecture, synthesizable RTL, testbench, lint/CDC findings, synthesis reporting and integration material as agreed.

Can you work on existing RTL?

Yes. Existing blocks can be extended, parameterized, restructured or remediated for reuse.

How is sensitive source handled?

Sensitive specifications and RTL can be exchanged under NDA with access scoped to the engagement.
