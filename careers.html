Who fits well here

Engineers who want depth in digital design, verification, FPGA and mathematically intensive hardware—not a role limited to one narrow ticket queue.

Engineering paths

Work where design decisions become real hardware.

Zepto Logic’s current work spans reusable IP, customer-facing engineering and applied R&D. Relevant profiles can be considered as programme needs and approved positions develop.

RTL

Digital design & microarchitecture

Translate requirements and algorithms into reusable RTL, with ownership of interfaces, coding quality, synthesis and integration context.

Languages: Verilog · SystemVerilog · VHDL
Verification

UVM & verification engineering

Build verification plans and environments that expose real defects, drive regression and move coverage toward a defensible closure position.

Methods: UVM · assertions · formal
FPGA

Prototype & hardware validation

Take RTL onto AMD/Xilinx platforms through constraints, timing closure, bring-up and repeatable demonstration.

Platforms: Zynq · UltraScale+ · Alveo class
Research

Applied cryptography & secure hardware

Work on arithmetic, cryptographic acceleration, secure-computing blocks and other hardware where the mathematics is part of the engineering.

Focus: specialised digital hardware
