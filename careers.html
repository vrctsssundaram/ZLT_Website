Semiconductor work where an engineer can follow a block from architecture to hardware.

Zepto Logic's engineering work spans reusable IP, RTL, verification, FPGA prototyping and applied hardware research from its Coimbatore design centre.

Commercial separation

Careers remains a separate route from customer and technical-enquiry funnels. Recruitment calls to action do not appear on the commercial pages.

Engineering disciplines / 01

Where semiconductor engineers contribute.

Specific vacancies are published only after internal approval. The areas below describe the engineering disciplines represented in the company's work, not a promise that every role is currently open.

RTL

Digital design & microarchitecture

Reusable IP, block architecture, synthesizable RTL, lint and CDC/RDC quality work.

Languages: Verilog · SystemVerilog · VHDL
Verification

UVM & verification engineering

Verification planning, constrained-random environments, regression, coverage and defect analysis.

Methods: UVM · assertions · formal
FPGA

Prototype & hardware validation

AMD/Xilinx implementation, constraints, timing closure, bring-up and demonstration.

Platforms: Zynq · UltraScale+ · Alveo class
Research

Applied cryptography & secure hardware

Hardware research around cryptographic compute, security and specialised arithmetic.

Focus: mathematically intensive hardware
