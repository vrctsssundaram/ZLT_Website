Work where architecture, RTL and verification decisions remain visible.

Engineers at Zepto Logic contribute to real design artefacts: microarchitectures, RTL blocks, verification environments, FPGA implementations, reusable IP and applied research hardware. The expectation is technical reasoning that can be explained, reviewed and improved.

Engineering disciplines

Several routes into the same semiconductor mission.

Openings vary with programmes, but the company builds capability around these technical disciplines.

Digital design

Microarchitecture, Verilog/SystemVerilog/VHDL, interfaces, arithmetic datapaths, lint and CDC/RDC.

Design verification

Verification planning, UVM, assertions, constrained random, regression, debugging and coverage closure.

FPGA engineering

AMD FPGA implementation, constraints, timing closure, board bring-up and repeatable hardware demonstration.

Semiconductor IP

Arithmetic and interface IP designed to be verified, integrated, documented and supported as reusable assets.

Applied R&D

Cryptographic acceleration, secure hardware, modular arithmetic and compute-intensive design problems.

Technical ownership

Clear reasoning, disciplined documentation and responsibility for taking a design decision through review and evidence.

Internships are structured around engineering contribution.

B.E. and M.E. VLSI candidates can enter through technical screening and an engineering discussion. Selected interns work against defined technical outputs with review, documentation and measurable progress.

Technical screening

Digital/VLSI fundamentals, HDL understanding and problem solving.

Engineering discussion

Reasoning about architecture, RTL, verification and implementation trade-offs.

Project contribution

Defined outputs reviewed against the engineering objective.
