The work is close enough to the hardware that engineering judgment matters.

Zepto Logic is built around small technical teams, reusable IP and direct ownership of semiconductor workstreams. That means engineers are expected to understand the requirement, make architectural decisions, write and review RTL, interpret verification evidence and follow the design toward hardware proof.

This is not a role list pretending to be a culture statement. It is the kind of work the company does.

Where engineers contribute

Work that can become a real engineering asset.

The exact opening mix changes with programmes. These are the disciplines around which Zepto Logic builds technical capability.

RTL

Digital design

Microarchitecture, Verilog/SystemVerilog/VHDL, interfaces, arithmetic datapaths, lint and CDC/RDC.

DV

Design verification

Verification planning, UVM, assertions, constrained random, regression, debugging and coverage closure.

FPGA

Hardware prototyping

AMD FPGA implementation, constraints, timing closure, board bring-up and repeatable demonstration.

IP

Reusable hardware

Arithmetic and interface IP designed to be reviewed, verified, integrated and supported.

R&D

Applied research

Cryptographic acceleration, secure hardware, modular arithmetic and other compute-heavy design problems.

OWN

Technical ownership

Clear reasoning, documentation and the discipline to carry a decision from requirement to evidence.
