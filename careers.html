Careers

Build digital hardware from architecture to FPGA evidence.

Engineering opportunities span RTL design, verification, FPGA implementation, reusable IP and applied hardware R&D at Zepto Logic’s engineering centre in Coimbatore.

Engineering disciplines

Work across the digital design stack.

Openings vary with active work, but technical hiring is centred on digital design, verification, FPGA engineering and specialised hardware development.

Digital design

Architecture + RTL

Microarchitecture, Verilog/SystemVerilog/VHDL, interfaces, arithmetic datapaths, lint and CDC/RDC.

Verification

UVM + coverage

Verification planning, assertions, constrained random, regression, debugging and coverage closure.

FPGA

Implementation + bring-up

AMD FPGA constraints, timing closure, integration and repeatable hardware demonstration.

Applied R&D

Specialised hardware

Cryptographic acceleration, secure processing, arithmetic engines and compute-intensive design research.

Engineering environment

Technical ownership with reviewable outputs.

Work is organised around design artefacts and evidence: specifications, microarchitectures, RTL, verification environments, FPGA implementations, reports and integration notes.

01
