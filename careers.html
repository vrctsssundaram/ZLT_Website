Careers

Work on semiconductor IP, RTL, verification and FPGA systems.

Engineering roles and internships are centred on digital hardware design, implementation and verification. The engineering centre is in Coimbatore, India.

Engineering disciplines

Work close to the hardware.

Projects span reusable IP, custom digital blocks, verification environments, FPGA implementation and applied hardware research.

Digital design

Microarchitecture, Verilog, SystemVerilog, VHDL, interfaces, arithmetic datapaths and integration.

Design verification

Verification planning, UVM, assertions, constrained random, regression, debugging and coverage closure.

FPGA engineering

AMD FPGA implementation, constraints, timing closure, board bring-up and hardware validation.

Semiconductor IP

Design, verification, documentation and integration of reusable arithmetic and interface IP.

Applied R&D

Cryptographic acceleration, secure hardware, modular arithmetic and specialised digital compute.

Engineering quality

Technical reasoning, review discipline, documentation and ownership of implementation details.
