Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited

A semiconductor IP and engineering company.

Founded in 2018, Zepto Logic develops reusable digital IP, front-end semiconductor engineering and applied hardware R&D from its engineering centre in Coimbatore, India.

Reusable hardware assets, focused engineering and research-led technology development.

The operating portfolio combines FPGA-validated arithmetic and interface IP with architecture, RTL design, verification, lint, CDC/RDC, FPGA prototyping and IP-readiness engineering.

Applied R&D extends this base across cryptographic acceleration, post-quantum hardware, secure processing and specialised digital architectures.
