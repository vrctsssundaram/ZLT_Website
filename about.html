Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited

Incorporated on 18 December 2018. Semiconductor IP, front-end VLSI engineering and applied hardware R&D from Tamil Nadu, India.

Discuss a programme →
We build reusable hardware assets and take on focused semiconductor workstreams.

Zepto Logic operates as an engineering company first. The commercial model combines internally developed semiconductor IP with architecture, RTL, verification and FPGA delivery for customers who need a specific technical result without building every capability in-house.

That makes the engagement practical: evaluate what already exists, commission what must be different, and keep the work centred on an engineering asset your team can inspect, verify and integrate.

Where we operate

A working engineering base in Coimbatore.

The company operates from TICEL Bio Park on Maruthamalai Road, with FPGA development capability, engineering workstations and front-end design infrastructure. The operating model is primarily offshore, with short onsite support in India where a programme needs it.
