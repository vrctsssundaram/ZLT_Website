Company

A fabless design house on a national mission.

Founded in Coimbatore in 2018, Zepto Logic builds indigenous semiconductor IP and full-stack design capability for India and the world.

Genesis

We build the IP.
Foundries build the silicon.

Zepto Logic was founded in 2018 as part of India's push to design chips at home, not merely assemble them. Our work spans processor cores, interface protocols, analog blocks and security engines — engineered in India and licensable globally.

The company operates as a fabless, foundry-agnostic design house, combining reusable semiconductor IP with end-to-end design services.
