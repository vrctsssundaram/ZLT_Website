Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited

Incorporated on 18 December 2018. Engineering-led, Indian-owned and focused on semiconductor IP, VLSI design capability and applied hardware R&D.

Why the company exists
Indigenous IPEngineering depthSpecialised hardwareCommercial execution

Build semiconductor capability that remains valuable after the project ends.

Zepto Logic develops reusable hardware assets, executes semiconductor engineering programmes and advances applied R&D around secure and compute-intensive digital systems. The commercial model starts with capabilities that are ready now and compounds them into owned IP, reusable methods and a broader Indian semiconductor capability base.

Owned IP, front-end execution and a longer semiconductor roadmap.

The operating business today combines FPGA-validated arithmetic and interface IP with architecture, RTL design, UVM verification, lint, CDC/RDC, FPGA prototyping and IP-readiness engineering.

Alongside that commercial base, the company is developing capability across ASIC synthesis, formal and low-power work, DFT and timing disciplines, secure computing, cryptographic acceleration and future semiconductor infrastructure.

Engineering base

Coimbatore is the operating centre.

Zepto Logic operates from TICEL Bio Park on Maruthamalai Road with FPGA development capability, engineering workstations and front-end design infrastructure.
