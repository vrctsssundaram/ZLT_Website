Who we are

An engineering perspective.
An open approach.

Zepto Logic Technologies works in semiconductor engineering, digital hardware and applied research. We welcome discussions with organisations seeking consultancy, development support or collaboration around a defined requirement.

Our approach is to understand the application and working context before proposing how an engagement might be structured.

Purpose

Make technical conversations useful.

Connect the question being asked with the engineering decisions that follow.

Approach

Keep the requirement at the centre.

Discuss scope, constraints and expectations with the people responsible for the programme.

Working principles

Clarity. Care. Collaboration.

Respect confidentiality, communicate precisely and agree responsibilities before work begins.

Leadership

Suresh Kuppuswamy

Co-Founder, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer

Suresh’s leadership experience spans technology services, financial infrastructure, enterprise transformation and corporate strategy.

His career includes responsibilities across India, Europe and the Middle East, with organisations including Tata Consultancy Services, IBM, CAMS/SSPL, Adrenalin eSystems and the Sakthi Group.

He is a Harvard Business School Advanced Management Program alumnus and holds a B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering from Coimbatore Institute of Technology.
