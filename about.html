Built in Coimbatore to own IP and solve specialised semiconductor problems.

Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited develops semiconductor IP in-house and delivers front-end VLSI engineering across architecture, RTL, verification and FPGA prototyping. The company was incorporated in 2018 and operates from TICEL Bio Park, Coimbatore.
