Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited

Incorporated on 18 December 2018 and based in Coimbatore, India, with a focus on semiconductor IP, digital design engineering and applied hardware R&D.

Company principles
Owned IPEngineering depthSpecialised hardwareCommercial execution

Build hardware capability that compounds.

Zepto Logic combines reusable semiconductor IP, front-end engineering and applied R&D around secure and compute-intensive digital systems. The objective is not only to complete an assignment, but to create engineering assets, methods and hardware capability that retain value beyond it.

Reusable IP, front-end execution and a broader semiconductor horizon.

The operating business combines FPGA-validated arithmetic and interface IP with architecture, RTL design, UVM verification, lint, CDC/RDC, FPGA prototyping and IP-readiness engineering.

Alongside that base, Zepto Logic is expanding capability across selected ASIC front-end work, secure computing, cryptographic acceleration and future semiconductor infrastructure.

Engineering base

Coimbatore is the operating centre.

The TICEL Bio Park engineering base includes FPGA development capability, engineering workstations and front-end design infrastructure.
