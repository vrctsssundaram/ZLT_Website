Buyer FAQ

Questions to settle before evaluation.

01

How is the IP delivered?

Synthesizable soft IP under a written commercial license, with collateral defined for the selected function.

02

Can the arithmetic be customized?

Yes—when the required precision, interface or datapath differs, the requirement moves into custom RTL engineering.

03

Is it tied to a foundry?

The public positioning is technology-independent RTL; implementation results must be qualified against the target device or ASIC context.

04

Can evaluation move under NDA?

Yes. Sensitive source, architecture and detailed characterization can be exchanged after an NDA is in place.

05

What should we send first?

The required function, precision, target, interface context and the decision the evaluation needs to support.
