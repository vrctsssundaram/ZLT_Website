Buyer FAQ

Questions to answer before selecting the prototype path.

01

What can we bring as input?

RTL, an algorithm or reference model, a target board or a design that still needs platform selection.

02

Which FPGA family do you primarily support?

The primary current environment is AMD, formerly Xilinx, with Vivado/Vitis and Zynq/UltraScale+/Alveo-class platforms.

03

What can the hand-off include?

Ported design, constraints, timing evidence, bitstream, bring-up notes and a repeatable demonstration path.

04

Can you help with fit and timing problems?

Yes. Resource pressure, target selection, constraints and implementation behavior can be reviewed.

05

Is FPGA validation the same as silicon validation?

No. FPGA validation is hardware evidence on a programmable target and must not be represented as ASIC post-silicon validation.
