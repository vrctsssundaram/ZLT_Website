Reusable IP review & integration

Assess a reusable hardware design block, also called semiconductor IP, before integrating it into your system.

Ask about this service

What we can help with

Design review

Check available documentation, interface specifications and verification evidence.

Interface fit

Review data formats, timing, clock and reset behaviour and integration assumptions.

Integration plan

Identify work needed to adapt the block, verify the integration and document any open issues.

What to share when you contact us

A short, non-confidential description is enough to start.

  • The block and its intended use
  • The interfaces and documentation you can describe
  • Your integration stage and acceptance criteria
