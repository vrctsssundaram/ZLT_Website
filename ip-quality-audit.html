Buyer FAQ

Questions to answer before an inherited block is trusted as IP.

01

What RTL is suitable?

Internal reusable blocks, research-grade RTL, inherited IP and customer-owned designs can all be reviewed.

02

What does the audit inspect?

Lint/code quality, CDC/RDC, interfaces, clocks/resets, parameters, constraints, documentation and synthesis observations.

03

Does this replace functional verification?

No. It is a front-end quality and integration-readiness review, not a substitute for verification or physical sign-off.

04

What comes back?

Prioritized findings, integration gaps and a recommended remediation sequence.

05

Can Zepto Logic remediate the findings?

Yes. RTL, documentation, verification or FPGA follow-on work can be scoped separately.
