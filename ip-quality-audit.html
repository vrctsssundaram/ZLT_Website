A front-end quality review for RTL another team needs to trust and reuse.

Lint · CDC/RDC · integration readiness

Structural quality, crossing assumptions, documentation, synthesis observations and reuse-readiness findings in one engineering review.

Reuse readiness depends on more than functional simulation.

The review examines the engineering asset as its next integrator will experience it: code quality, clock/reset crossings, interface contracts, parameters, constraints, documentation and synthesis observations.

The result is a prioritized readiness view for remediation before hand-off or wider reuse.

Review scope

Structural quality, crossing discipline and integration clarity.

Internal reusable RTL, research-grade blocks, inherited IP and customer-owned designs can all enter the review.

Structural quality

Lint, coding consistency, maintainability and synthesis observations relevant to reuse.

Clock and reset crossings

Domain structure, synchronization strategy, exceptions and areas requiring engineering review.

Integration contract

Interfaces, clocks, resets, parameters, constraints and documentation needed for a clean hand-off.

Output

A concise readiness view with a remediation sequence.

Findings are ranked by integration impact, reuse risk and maintainability so the work can proceed in the right order.

Prioritized findings

Issues grouped by technical impact and reuse consequence.

Integration gaps

Missing or ambiguous interface, clock/reset, parameter, constraint and documentation information.

Remediation sequence

An ordered set of changes for the owning team or a separately scoped Zepto Logic engineering workstream.

Technical questions

Scope, outputs and relationship to functional verification.

What RTL is suitable?

Internal reusable blocks, research-grade RTL, inherited IP and customer-owned designs can all be reviewed.

What does the review inspect?

Lint/code quality, CDC/RDC, interfaces, clocks/resets, parameters, constraints, documentation and synthesis observations.

How does this relate to functional verification?

The review focuses on front-end quality and integration readiness. Functional verification is a separate block-specific engineering activity and can be scoped alongside it when required.

What does the review return?

Prioritized findings, integration gaps and an ordered remediation sequence.

Can Zepto Logic implement the remediation?

Yes. RTL, documentation, verification or FPGA follow-on work can be scoped after the readiness review.

Make reuse a deliberate engineering decision.

Share the RTL scope, intended integration context and existing verification or synthesis material. The review can focus on the issues that matter to the next team.
