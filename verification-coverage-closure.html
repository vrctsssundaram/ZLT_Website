Common questions

What to know before the audit starts.

What is a verification-readiness audit?

A focused review of the specification, RTL, verification plan, environment, regression behaviour and coverage position to identify the highest-value closure actions.

Can you use our existing UVM environment?

Yes. Existing infrastructure is reviewed first; replacement is not assumed.

Can verification be independent from design?

Yes. Independent verification can provide a separate engineering view of internally developed RTL.

What should we send first?

The specification, RTL, verification plan, recent regression evidence, coverage reports and known defect history are useful starting inputs.

Can source move under NDA?

Yes. Detailed RTL and verification source can be exchanged after an NDA is in place.
