Technical questions

Inputs, outputs and confidentiality.

What is a verification-readiness review?

A focused review of the specification, RTL, verification plan, environment, regression behaviour and coverage position to identify the highest-value closure actions.

Can you work with our existing UVM environment?

Yes. Existing infrastructure is reviewed in its current form and follow-on changes are scoped around the actual gaps found.

Can verification be independent from design?

Yes. Independent verification provides a separate engineering view of internally developed RTL and its verification evidence.

What should we send first?

The specification, RTL, verification plan, recent regression evidence, coverage reports and known defect history are useful starting inputs.

How is sensitive source handled?

Detailed RTL and verification source can be exchanged under NDA with access limited to the engagement.

A coverage report is useful when it leads to an engineering decision.

Share the current environment, recent regression evidence and the closure target. Zepto Logic can begin with an independent review or a defined verification workstream.
