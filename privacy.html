Privacy Policy

How Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited handles personal information received through this website and related enquiry channels.

Last updated: 25 August 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through the Zepto Logic website, technical and commercial enquiries, research or partnership enquiries, and recruitment-related contact. Personal data is handled subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable.

2. Information we may collect

Information you provide may include your name, work or institutional email address, company or institution, country, job title, phone number where supplied, project category, technical enquiry text, NDA preference and recruitment information.

Please do not submit confidential RTL, source code, protected invention details, customer data or other sensitive technical material before an appropriate NDA and secure exchange route are in place.

3. Website and device information

Hosting and security infrastructure may record technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested pages and timestamps for security, reliability and diagnostics. Where non-essential analytics or similar tracking requires consent, it will be enabled in accordance with applicable requirements.

4. How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, evaluate potential business or research engagements, route requests to relevant personnel, process recruitment enquiries, maintain website security and reliability, improve website performance, maintain appropriate business records and comply with legal obligations.

5. Internal access and routing

Enquiries may be shared internally with technical, business, management or recruitment personnel according to the subject of the request. Access is limited according to business need and the purpose for which the information was provided.

6. Confidential technical material

Initial website enquiries should contain non-confidential context only. Where detailed design information is required, Zepto Logic can establish an NDA and an approved exchange method before sensitive material is transferred.

7. Disclosure to third parties

Zepto Logic does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers supporting website or business operations under appropriate obligations, to professional advisers, or where disclosure is required by law or a competent authority.

8. Retention

Personal information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, business relationship, recruitment process, security purpose, legal obligation or record-keeping need for which it was collected.

9. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal information held about you, or raise a concern about its handling. Requests may be sent to info@zeptologic.com.

11. Children

This website is intended for business, professional, research, academic and employment audiences and is not designed for children.

12. External links

External websites linked from this site operate under their own privacy practices. Zepto Logic is not responsible for their content or privacy policies.

13. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website functionality, business processes or applicable requirements change. The revision date shown above identifies the current published version.

14. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to info@zeptologic.com or by post to Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited, TICEL Bio Park Ltd, M:503, 5th Floor, Maruthamalai Road, Somayampalayam, Coimbatore – 641046, Tamil Nadu, India.
