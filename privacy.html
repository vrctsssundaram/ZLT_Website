Privacy Policy

How Zepto Logic Technologies handles information submitted through its website and technical enquiry routes.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited (“Zepto Logic”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information collected through this website and related enquiry processes. The policy is intended to operate consistently with applicable Indian data-protection requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as applicable.

2. Information you provide

When you contact us, we may receive information such as your name, work email address, company or institution, country, job title, phone number where provided, project category, technical enquiry text and your NDA preference.

Please do not submit confidential RTL, source code, protected invention details, customer data or other sensitive technical information before an appropriate NDA and secure exchange route are in place.

3. Technical and usage information

Standard hosting infrastructure may record technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and timestamps for security, reliability and diagnostics. If analytics or session-measurement tools are introduced, any consent required by applicable law will be requested before non-essential tracking is enabled.

4. How we use information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, route technical requests to the appropriate internal owner, evaluate potential business or research engagements, manage recruitment enquiries, maintain website security, improve website performance and comply with legal obligations.

5. Enquiry routing

Different categories of enquiry may be routed internally to technical, business, management or recruitment personnel according to the subject of the request. Access should be limited to people who need the information for the stated purpose.

6. Confidential technical material

Initial website enquiries are intended for non-confidential project context. Where detailed design material is required, Zepto Logic can establish an NDA and an approved secure exchange route before sensitive files are transferred.

7. Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support website or business operations under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations, with professional advisers, or where disclosure is required by law or a competent authority.

8. Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, relationship, legal requirement, security purpose or record-keeping need for which it was collected.

9. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction or deletion of personal information held about you, or raise a concern about its handling. Requests may be sent to info@zeptologic.com.

11. Children

This website is directed to business, professional, research and employment audiences and is not designed for children.

12. External links

External websites linked from this site operate under their own privacy practices. Zepto Logic is not responsible for their content or privacy policies.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated as website functionality, law or internal processes change. The version published here is the current website version.

14. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to info@zeptologic.com or by post to Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited, TICEL Bio Park Ltd, M:503, 5th Floor, Maruthamalai Road, Somayampalayam, Coimbatore – 641046, Tamil Nadu, India.
