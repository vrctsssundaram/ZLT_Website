Privacy policy.

How Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited handles personal information received through this website and related enquiry channels.

Scope

This policy applies to personal information collected through the Zepto Logic website, technical and commercial enquiries, research or partnership enquiries, and recruitment-related contact. Personal data is handled subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable.

Information you provide

Information may include your name, work or institutional email address, company or institution, country, phone number where supplied, project category, technical enquiry text, NDA preference and recruitment information.

Please do not submit confidential RTL, source code, protected invention details, customer data or other sensitive technical material before an appropriate NDA and secure exchange route are in place.

Website and device information

Hosting and security infrastructure may record technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested pages and timestamps for security, reliability and diagnostics. The enquiry service stores a privacy-preserving source hash rather than a raw visitor IP for basic abuse prevention.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, evaluate potential business or research engagements, route requests to relevant personnel, process recruitment enquiries, maintain website security and reliability, improve site performance, maintain appropriate business records and comply with legal obligations.

Internal access and confidential material

Enquiries may be shared internally with technical, business, management or recruitment personnel according to the subject of the request. Initial website enquiries should contain non-confidential context only. Where detailed design information is required, Zepto Logic can establish an NDA and an approved exchange method before sensitive material is transferred.

Third parties

Zepto Logic does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers supporting website or business operations under appropriate obligations, to professional advisers, or where disclosure is required by law or a competent authority.

Retention and security

Personal information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, business relationship, recruitment process, security purpose, legal obligation or record-keeping need for which it was collected. Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information, although no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal information held about you, or raise a concern about its handling. Contact info@zeptologic.com.

Children and external links

This website is intended for business, professional, research, academic and employment audiences and is not designed for children. External websites linked from this site operate under their own privacy practices.

Updates

This policy may be updated when website functionality, business processes or applicable requirements change. The revision date above identifies the current published version.
