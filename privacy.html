Privacy Notice

Website information for Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited.

Information you provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, email, organisation and message. Country and confidentiality preference are optional. Keep your first enquiry free of confidential designs, sensitive personal information and protected implementation details.

How we use information

Zepto Logic Technologies Private Limited uses the information you provide to respond to your enquiry and discuss a potential engagement.

Hosting and delivery

The website sends enquiries through a Supabase-hosted service. Our hosting and enquiry providers may process technical information to serve the website and handle requests. The site offers an email option when form delivery cannot be confirmed.

Cookies and caching

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing list. Your browser may cache site assets. Third-party websites linked from this site have their own practices.

Your choices

To ask about information you provided, or to request a correction or deletion, email info@zeptologic.com. Contact us to ask about retention or other applicable obligations.
