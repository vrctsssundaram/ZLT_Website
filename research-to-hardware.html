A strong research result is not automatically a transferable hardware asset.

Another team needs interfaces, repeatability, documentation, verification and an implementation they can understand without the original researcher sitting beside them.

Zepto Logic helps universities and research teams build that engineering layer around promising digital-hardware work.

Where research usually stalls

Close the gap between a result and an asset.

The right next step depends on what is already real and what an evaluator, partner or future licensee needs to trust.

01

Architecture

Turn the algorithm or proof-of-concept into explicit interfaces, partitioning, assumptions and implementation intent.

02

RTL hardening

Move research code toward reusable, synthesizable and documented RTL another engineer can maintain.

03

Independent evidence

Create verification and FPGA proof that does not depend only on the original development environment.

Build the transfer path

Make the next evaluator’s job easier.

A partner or funding body should be able to understand what the design does, how it is checked, where it runs and what remains before the next stage.
