Turn a research result into hardware another organisation can evaluate, reproduce and extend.

Research hardening + technology transfer

Architecture, reusable RTL, independent verification and FPGA evidence for promising digital-hardware research.

Transferable hardware needs a clear engineering layer around the originating idea.

Interfaces, implementation assumptions, repeatable verification, documentation and a reproducible hardware path make a research result easier for a partner, funding body or future licensee to evaluate.

Zepto Logic works with academic and industrial research teams to build that layer around digital hardware and compute-intensive algorithms.

Transfer path

Architecture, reusable RTL and independent hardware evidence.

The next engineering stage is chosen from the maturity of the current research and the evidence required by the next evaluator.

Define the hardware architecture

Make interfaces, partitioning, performance intent, precision, clocks, resets and implementation assumptions explicit.

Harden RTL and verification

Develop synthesizable, documented RTL and repeatable verification collateral another engineer can review and maintain.

Demonstrate on FPGA

Use an appropriate AMD platform when programmable-hardware evidence is the right technical gate.
