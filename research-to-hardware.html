Home / R&D / Research-to-hardware

A research result is not yet an industrial hardware deliverable.

For universities and research laboratories, Zepto Logic can help translate a research-grade digital design into a clearer architecture, reusable RTL, verification collateral and FPGA evidence without presenting the work as commercial silicon.

First principle

IP ownership and publication boundaries should be agreed before engineering work begins.

Gap to close

Research proof → engineering evidence.

The technical gap is usually not the idea. It is repeatability, documentation, verification, interfaces, implementation quality and a hardware demonstration another organisation can evaluate.

A-01The institution has a working model but no industrial RTL path.Architecture review · synthesizable implementation•
A-02The design works once, but nobody can reproduce or verify it independently.Verification plan · self-checking environment · regression•
A-03The research output needs FPGA proof before a funding or transfer decision.Platform selection · implementation · demonstration•
A-04The technology may be commercialisable, but the IP boundary is unclear.Agree ownership and disclosure before engineering starts•
