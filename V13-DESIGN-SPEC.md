# V13 — Hybrid Pre-Nexus Semiconductor System

## Intent

V13 returns to the stronger V10 semiconductor atmosphere while retaining the factual, stature and programme-proof improvements developed after it.

## Required experience

- Dark navy semiconductor hero with Instrument Sans + JetBrains Mono.
- No persona selector, relationship-mode bar, routing console, opportunity console or recommended-route widget.
- Static hero proof architecture instead of a buyer-routing UI.
- Capability architecture that shows owned IP, current engineering, applied R&D, capability development and strategic roadmap.
- Maturity-aware semiconductor design flow.
- Real programme photography and public institutional milestones.
- 13-core authoritative IP portfolio.
- Direct technical contact, search, responsive navigation, enquiry backend and mobile contact dock.

## Explicit exclusions

The following V11/V12 Nexus mechanisms must not return:

- `nexus-bar`
- `nexus-console`
- `data-audience-choice`
- `audienceData`
- `ecoData`
- persona tabs
- "I am here to" / "Explore as" routing bars
- "Recommended route" panels

The V10 Silicon Workbench and Intent Lab are also not restored as-is. V13 uses V10 visual atmosphere, not V10's interactive routing widgets.

## Content hierarchy

1. Mission and indigenous semiconductor identity.
2. Public proof and company-scale commitments.
3. Current commercial capability.
4. Applied R&D and capability development.
5. Strategic roadmap.
6. Commercial contact after sufficient public information has been given.

## Truth boundary

Commercial capability, scoped capability, applied R&D, capability development and strategic roadmap must remain visibly distinct. Physical implementation, DFT production execution, analog/mixed-signal delivery and OSAT/package/test must not be presented as currently delivered production services.
