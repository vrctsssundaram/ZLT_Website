# V15 — Global Market Voice and Executive Semiconductor System

## Positioning principle
Zepto Logic is presented as a semiconductor IP and engineering company operating from Coimbatore, India and available for technical engagements across markets. Geography is factual company context; it is not the market definition.

Do not use nationality or origin as the primary commercial qualifier. Avoid phrases such as “Indian semiconductor IP”, “Indian-owned semiconductor IP”, “indigenous semiconductor IP” or “domestic semiconductor capability” in primary marketing language.

The India-focused search landing page may retain geographic search relevance, but its body must make clear that Coimbatore is the engineering base rather than an engagement boundary.

## Scale and evidence
Do not invent scale. Do not compensate for an early-stage portfolio with inflated claims.

The authoritative commercial portfolio remains 13 IP blocks: 9 arithmetic and 4 interface. These counts may remain in technical inventory, structural tests or detailed documentation where useful, but they should not be used as homepage stature metrics or global-market headline proof.

Primary public proof should favor capability, engineering ownership, hardware evidence and externally anchored milestones such as C-DOT Samarth selection and the Government of Tamil Nadu investment MoU.

## Voice
The site should read like an experienced semiconductor organisation speaking to another experienced organisation.

Use concise, native, executive language. State capability and evidence directly. Avoid narrating the sales logic, explaining why the website is credible, or repeatedly instructing the visitor how to qualify Zepto Logic.

Avoid lecture-like or defensive phrases such as:
- What you can engage today
- Know exactly where Zepto Logic can enter your programme
- Evaluate the engineering company behind the commercial offer
- See the proof before starting the commercial conversation
- Move a commercial requirement now
- Each commercial path has a concrete technical output

Use project, engagement, development, workstream, design, hardware effort or requirement according to context. Reserve “programme” primarily for official institutional names or factual public records such as C-DOT Samarth.

## Opportunity posture
Core capabilities must be explicit: semiconductor IP, architecture, RTL, verification, lint, CDC/RDC and FPGA prototyping.

Do not create artificial commercial walls around technically adjacent work. Selected ASIC front-end extensions and other technically aligned requirements may be assessed individually where the engineering fit is strong. This must not be phrased as a claim that undeveloped disciplines are already production-ready.

## Visual system
V15 retains the V14 light-only global-professional visual foundation.

Primary surfaces remain white, neutral gray and silver. Dark values are for text, not page or card backgrounds. Accent use is restrained cobalt, teal and metallic gold.

The homepage adds a light semiconductor signal-map visual: technical grid, signal traces and IP / RTL / DV / FPGA nodes converging on Zepto Logic. Motion is subtle and functional, with prefers-reduced-motion support. No neon, gaming aesthetic, stock laboratory imagery, fabricated facilities, generic AI-chip art or decorative dark-tech backgrounds.

## Commercial interaction
Primary wording is calm and direct: Explore semiconductor IP, Engineering, Start a discussion, Contact.

The contact experience begins with system, stage, constraint and outcome. It should not ask for budget, expose protected technical detail before NDA, or present the form as a qualification gate.

## Accuracy and disclosure
All Website 2.0 publication controls remain binding.

Never claim silicon validation, tapeout, patent-pending status, foundry readiness, unsupported customers, unsupported product deployment, production physical-design/DFT capability, or unapproved performance/PPA numbers.

Detailed PPA and protected architectural detail remain confidential. Public institutional milestones must preserve exact status language such as “proposed investment”, “grant sanctioned; fund release awaited”, and “sponsor board validation in progress” where applicable.

## Regression rules
Automated QA should fail if primary site copy reintroduces India-limiting positioning, old defensive sales-language patterns, or homepage headline use of the small portfolio counts.
