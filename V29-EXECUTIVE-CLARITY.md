# V29 — Executive Clarity

V29 responds to leadership review that the Zepto Logic website must explain the company in simple semiconductor language, make the offering immediately orderable/engageable, and stop presenting content as a visual catalogue without a direct commercial message.

## Primary site structure

The primary navigation is intentionally reduced to:

- Home
- IP
- Services
- R&D
- Company
- Contact

Applications and Insights remain available as supporting content and through contextual links/search, but they no longer compete with the three principal commercial/technical routes in the primary navigation.

## Homepage statement

The homepage leads directly with:

**Semiconductor IP. Engineering services. Applied R&D.**

The visitor can immediately choose one of three routes:

1. Semiconductor IP
2. Engineering Services
3. Applied R&D

## Direct offering menu

V29 explicitly names the work a customer can engage Zepto Logic for:

- IP licensing & evaluation
- Semiconductor consultation
- Architecture & RTL design
- Design verification
- FPGA prototyping & validation
- Collaborative R&D

## Services page

The Services page is organised around direct deliverables instead of a long narrative:

- Architecture & specification
- RTL design
- Design verification
- FPGA prototyping
- IP quality & reuse readiness
- Custom accelerator engineering

It also contains dedicated **Consultation** and **Design & prototyping lab** sections.

## Engagement vocabulary retained by leadership request

- Co-creation
- Collaboration
- Co-development
- Co-engineering
- Co-opting specialist capability

These are used only where they describe a real engagement model rather than as decorative marketing terminology.

## Positioning

V29 uses simple customer-oriented language: chip/product teams can use reusable IP to reduce rebuild effort, engage a focused semiconductor engineering service, request specialised consultation, or collaborate on applied R&D. The company is presented as a fabless semiconductor design enterprise in Coimbatore, India, with direct technical access and a cost-effective engagement structure.

## Visual direction

V29 continues the V28 white-first boardroom visual system. It does not reintroduce video, cinematic/cyberpunk presentation, Matrix-like effects, decorative motion controls, or unnecessary visual spectacle. The five-stage engineering path is visually reduced to a conventional review strip.

## Release discipline

Staging remains `noindex,nofollow`. Production remains a separate controlled migration. All existing content-claim, phone-isolation, CEO-profile, enquiry, accessibility, cross-browser, zoom/orientation and production-package gates remain in force.
