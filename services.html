Engineering method

Define. Build. Verify. Prove. Deliver.

A compact development path around the technical contract and measurable evidence.

Define the boundary

Interfaces, clocks, resets, parameterisation, platform, performance intent and verification expectations.

Build against evidence

RTL, testbench, coverage, synthesis, timing or FPGA behaviour according to the agreed scope.

Hand off usable engineering assets

Source, test collateral, reports and integration notes structured for the next stage of the design.
